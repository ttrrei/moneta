## Hypothesis and relationship

TR-05 supplies entry direction; candle_confirmation with immediate may filter weak entries, and may fail through delay, costs or regime change.

Source: TR-05; style: `trend`; score: `trend_age`;
original target: `directed_return`; transform: `identity`;
event kind: `tails`; tail: `high`.

New catalogue feature/context and exact hourly execution; differs from prior S-series raw-indicator rules.

Parent: original fifty-idea feature. Semantic hash: `504ed5d352bffb44b7545bab1dc95c04e2127872e524c9cec984d1d21a5fe330`.

This is a related composite entry hypothesis, not a newly independent primitive indicator.

## Executable contract

Read the exact completed 5m feature snapshot at a completed native 1h close.
Hourly context must be complete and less than one hour old. Early covariate
calibration ends 2020-07-01; no position opens earlier. Gaps and known
near-close clocks block entries. Future fill-bar completeness is not consulted.

Initial setup direction is `ltf_direction * 1`.
Require completed candle body in the chosen direction and hourly efficiency at least its early-calibration median.
Categorical sources use exact 1 for the high tail or 0 for the low tail;
continuous sources use the early upper/lower tail cut below. CB-01 also
requires positive squeeze age. The score transform is applied first.
Enter on a fresh qualifying setup, including a fresh direction change.

One unit long or short; no pyramiding or direct flips. Freeze ATR24 at entry.
Close-confirmed stop 1.0 ATR; profit 2.0 ATR;
maximum 24 observed hourly bars; cooldown 6 observations.
Fill decisions at the following native open. Force-close the final open position
at the final candle close. Observed bars may span weekend/session gaps.

Shared-engine shorts use reciprocal equity factors; the separate feature
study uses cash returns per entry notional. No financing/slippage model is included.
