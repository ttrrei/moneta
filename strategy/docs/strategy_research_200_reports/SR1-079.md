calibration ends 2020-07-01; no position opens earlier. Gaps and known
near-close clocks block entries. Future fill-bar completeness is not consulted.

Initial setup direction is the higher-timeframe trend, also confirmed by the six-observation return direction.
Require agreement with higher-timeframe direction.
Categorical sources use exact 1 for the high tail or 0 for the low tail;
continuous sources use the early upper/lower tail cut below. CB-01 also
requires positive squeeze age. The score transform is applied first.
Enter on a fresh qualifying setup, including a fresh direction change.

One unit long or short; no pyramiding or direct flips. Freeze ATR24 at entry.
Close-confirmed stop 1.0 ATR; profit 2.0 ATR;
maximum 24 observed hourly bars; cooldown 6 observations.
Fill decisions at the following native open. Force-close the final open position
at the final candle close. Observed bars may span weekend/session gaps.
